    Sizing

    Find your fit

    Made to order in women's and men's sizes. Bracelets sit snug with a little give; necklaces are strung to a standard 20" (custom lengths welcome).

    How to measure

    • 1
      Wrap a strip of paper

      Wrap a flexible tape or thin strip of paper around your wrist, just below the wrist bone.

    • 2
      Mark where it meets

      Mark where the end overlaps, then lay it flat against a ruler to get your measurement.

    • 3
      Add a quarter inch

      Add about ¼" for a comfortable fit, then match it to the chart. Between sizes? Size up.

    Need a custom length? Note it when you order — we string to your exact measurement at no extra cost.

    How to measure

    Wrap a flexible tape or strip of paper around your wrist just below the wrist bone. Mark where it overlaps, lay it flat against a ruler, then add about ¼" for a comfortable fit.

    Between sizes? Size up — beaded pieces sit best with a little room. Roll it on rather than pulling it, and it will hold its shape for years.

    Need a custom length? Note it when you order and we'll string it to your measurement at no extra cost.

    How to wear it

    Care

    Remove before swimming, showering or exercise. Wipe with a soft dry cloth after wear.

    Avoid perfume, lotion and household cleaners — natural stone is porous and loses polish with exposure. Store away from direct sunlight.

    Choose your stone size

    6mm — a slimmer, understated bead. Sits lower on the wrist and layers well with a watch or a second piece.

    8mm — a bolder bead with more presence. Reads as a standalone statement and shows off the stone's facets.

    Both use the same genuine stone and durable cord — it's purely a question of proportion.

    Make it personal

    Every piece is made to order, so it can be built to you:

    · Initials or a charm — add an initial bead, cross, crown or accent.
    · Custom length — we'll string to your exact measurement at no extra cost.
    · Stone swaps — request a specific stone or a mix from another piece.

    Add your request in the personalization field, and we'll confirm details and pricing by email before we begin.
